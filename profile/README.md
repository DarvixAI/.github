## What Darvix AI does

Darvix AI gives a small business a team of six AI employees. You don't operate them like tools: they do the work, and you approve it.

| AI employee | Role | What they handle |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Alex** | Chief of Staff | Plans the week and hands work to the rest of the team |
| **Mark** | Web | Builds and updates your website, SEO and AI search visibility |
| **Rae** | Store | Runs your online store: products, orders and payments |
| **Lex** | Social | Writes, schedules and publishes posts, and reports what worked |
| **Jenny** | Support | Answers WhatsApp, Instagram and website chats, runs your CRM and campaigns |
| **Nova** | Assistant | Your personal assistant for everything else |

## Everything in one place

- **Unified inbox**: WhatsApp, Instagram DMs and website chat in one inbox, answered by AI
- **CRM**: every customer, conversation, booking and order on one record
- **Bookings**: online appointments, with optional payment upfront
- **Online store**: sell products with Stripe or Razorpay, 0% commission
- **Campaigns**: email from your own domain and WhatsApp broadcasts, written by AI
- **Social media**: a content calendar for every platform, with analytics
- **Forms**: designed forms with logic, file uploads and signatures that feed your CRM
- **Reviews**: automatic review requests after a visit or order
- **AI creative studio**: images, UGC-style video ads and print designs for your brand
